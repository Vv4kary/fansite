Biography

Cristiano Ronaldo (CR7) is widely regarded as one of the greatest forwards in football history. Driven by relentless discipline and unmatched athleticism, he redefined what it means to be a complete goalscorer—combining explosive power, aerial dominance, and clinical finishing to win five Ballon d'Or awards and shatter all-time scoring records in world football.
